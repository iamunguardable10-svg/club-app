# Product Vision

## Working name

Club App / TeamLoad OS

## One-sentence vision

Club App is a club operating system that helps admins, coaches and athletes organize teams, sessions, attendance, load and facilities in one connected platform.

## Product category

This is not only an attendance tool. It is a lightweight operating system for sports clubs and team environments.

## Primary users

### 1. Club Admin

The admin creates the club workspace and sets up the operating structure:

- club
- departments
- teams
- coaches
- roles
- facilities
- permissions

The admin should be actively guided by setup flows. They should not have to understand the entire data model manually.

### 2. Coach

The coach runs the daily team workflow:

- plan sessions
- invite athletes
- see who is coming
- see who is late, maybe or out
- understand reasons
- finalize attendance
- monitor load signals
- adjust training decisions

The coach interface must be decision-focused and not overloaded.

### 3. Athlete

The athlete uses the app quickly and frequently:

- see calendar
- report availability
- report late/maybe/no with reason
- submit RPE/load after sessions
- see personal load and participation trends
- receive relevant team context

Athlete flows must be mobile-first and low-friction.

## V1 product principle

V1 should be a correct skeleton, not a finished platform.

The goal is to build:

- correct information architecture
- correct role separation
- correct invite flows
- clean placeholder modules
- clear navigation
- scalable database assumptions

V1 does not need full analytics, AI, chat or advanced automation.

## Core promise

For coaches:

> Open the app and immediately know who is coming today, who is missing, who is late, why, and who needs load attention.

For athletes:

> Open the app and immediately know what is next, what needs to be reported, and what your current status is.

For admins:

> Open the app and immediately know whether the club structure, teams, roles and facilities are set up correctly.

## What the product is not

Club App is not:

- a generic calendar app
- a social network
- a simple attendance sheet
- a heavy enterprise ERP
- a medical record system
- a chat-first team app
- a data dashboard without workflow value

## Long-term direction

The system should eventually support multiple sports and departments, for example:

- Basketball
- Football
- Fencing
- Strength & Conditioning
- Youth academy departments
- performance units

But V1 should focus on a small, clean structure that can scale later.
