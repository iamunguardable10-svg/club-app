# Club OS audit — November 2026

Scope: commit `9d59e8b`, demo club in the browser, German, 390×844 and 1280×800,
all main pages as player, coach and club admin, plus the entry pages; code read
for the flows named in the brief; `supabase/pilot/migrations` surveyed for
row-level security, definer functions and grants, with carpools, consent and
parent consent read in full. No sign-in to and no write against the live project.

Checks: `typecheck`, `check:i18n`, `test:i18n`, `build` pass. `test:smoke` could
not run here (the repository's Playwright version has no matching browser in
this container); my own walk loaded every route of the smoke list at both
sizes: no page errors, no console errors, no sideways scroll, one `h1` each.
The database tests were not run (no local Postgres). Not covered: real sign-in,
e-mail, push, calendar feeds, the installed app on a real iPhone.

"Reproduced" means done in the demo; "code" means read, not run.

## 2. Improvements, best first

1. **Answer on the card (player).** Every upcoming session card carries
   "Dabei · Später · Absagen" as one segmented control; the chosen state is the
   card's chip. Declining opens four reasons, one tap. With "automatisch dabei"
   only "Absagen" shows. This is the action players perform most; it goes from
   four steps to one, and coaches get answers earlier. Covers findings 4, 6, 17.

2. **Player "Heute" in three blocks.** (a) Next: the next session, large, with
   its answer and, for games, the car. (b) To do: at most one item at a time
   (rate the last session, coach's question, finish the card), as one row with a
   count "1 von 3". (c) Below: the week as compact rows, then news. The three
   load tiles move to "Belastung", where they already are. The first screen then
   answers "what is next, what must I answer" as DESIGN.md says.

3. **One sheet, one row, two levels.** Build `os-sheet` and `os-row` once and
   move session details, rating, player details and the lists onto them; remove
   inner borders. This single change does most for "premium and calm": the
   weight comes from about 380 hand-built bordered boxes and all-bold text, not
   from the colours. Covers 11, 12, 13, and most of 10.

4. **The session as the centre.** The answer, the car, the squad, attendance
   and the rating all belong to one session, but live on "Heute", the
   calendar, the team overview and the history. Build one session view that
   moves with time and is reached from every place a session is shown:
   before (who comes, rides, meeting point), from the start (who is here),
   after (rating for the player; attendance, load and notes for the coach).
   The history then is simply the list of past sessions opening the same
   view, and every later feature (game statistics, video) has a place to go.

5. **Coach "Heute" as an attendance board.** For the next session: one line
   "9 kommen · 3 fehlen · 1 offen", then only the exceptions as rows, then
   "Offene erinnern" as the one action; the list is the session view of
   proposal 4, not a second one. The three "1 ohne Antwort · …" cards
   become one row "3 Einheiten mit offenen Antworten". From the start time the
   same block is "Wer war dabei?" with everyone ticked and one "Stimmt so". On desktop the freed right column shows the week.

6. **Calendar that opens where the day is.** Scroll to the first session;
   show 06:00–23:00 only when something is there; on the phone default to a
   week list (day headers, session rows) with the grid as the second view,
   since a youth team has three or four sessions a week and the grid is mostly
   empty. Tapping a free time opens the sheet directly (finding 3). Add
   "Wiederholen: wöchentlich bis …" to the sheet; today repetition is a
   separate mode ("Wochenplan") that a new coach will not find.

7. **Tabs for what is used daily.** Coach today: Heute, Kalender, Team,
   Hallen, Verlauf, with messages as an icon in the head. The team
   "Übersicht" repeats "Heute" (same session, same absence list); halls and
   history are opened rarely. Proposed: Heute · Kalender · Team · Nachrichten.
   Halls become a view inside the calendar ("nach Halle") and a group in the
   team options; history becomes the past in the calendar and a "Statistik"
   tab in the team; the team opens on the roster. Player: Heute · Kalender ·
   Belastung · Team · Nachrichten, where "Team" holds the playbook that is
   "Taktik" today, the coaches and the rides for the next game (players
   cannot read the roster today, `people_read` in `0040`; showing teammates
   would be a rights change and is not part of this). Messages then sit in the same place for
   every role (`RoleShell.tsx:37-57`). No function is removed, only moved;
   it is still a product decision for `docs/simplify-decisions.md`.

8. **Youth mode per team.** For teams under 14: load off by default (plan A5),
   the rating as five faces instead of 1–10 if a coach turns it on, no ACWR
   chips. For all under-16 players: the consent state is visible to the coach
   as one roster filter "Einwilligung fehlt (2)" with "Link erneut senden".

9. **Parents, the small version.** No parent accounts (Ben's decision stands),
   but the verified parent e-mail from finding 1 can receive the weekly plan and
   game-day details by mail, with an unsubscribe link. Parents of 10- to
   14-year-olds drive the cars and need the times.

10. **Honest load screen.** Top: one sentence and a traffic light ("Du kannst
   normal trainieren"). Below it the chart. "Spielraum, Monotonie,
   Beanspruchung, Abwechslung, Belastungsprofil" behind "Mehr Zahlen". The page
   is 2,759 px on the phone today.

11. **Messages as a feed.** The composer becomes one field at the bottom
   ("Nachricht an U16 Jungen…") that expands on focus to recipients, poll and
   "Wichtig"; the last recipient is remembered. Read counts and reminders move
   behind a tap on "Gelesen 3/12". Rows lose their border boxes.

12. **Club setup as a checklist.** `/club` opens with "Einrichtung 3 von 5"
    (departments, teams, head coaches invited, halls, players joined) and one
    next step; the structure below is a collapsed tree. A youth leader sees in
    one look which team still has no coach.

13. **Demo switch out of the content.** The full-width "DEMO Verein Trainer
    Spieler" bar is the first thing on every page and pushes content down by
    76 px. One pill in the page head.

14. **After joining: install and notifications in one step.** Plan B8: a
    single screen after the first join, "Damit du nichts verpasst": add to home
    screen (with the two iPhone steps shown), then allow notifications.

15. **Code health that pays.** `AthleteLoadWorkspace.tsx` (2,284 lines) is the
    player's home, calendar and load page in one file, and
    `CoachSessionSurfaces.tsx` / `CoachWorkspaceRouter.tsx` hold about 2,600
    lines; split by page when proposals 2, 4 and 5 are built. Add the smoke test's
    checks for text under 12 px and for truncated `h1`/subtitles, so findings
    10 and 15 cannot return. Pin the Playwright browser so `test:smoke` runs in
    any container.

## 3. What changed in DESIGN.md

- **Foundations** rewritten as a token system: surface and text levels with a
  contrast floor (`slate-400`), a seven-step type scale with weights and a
  12 px minimum, a spacing scale, three radii, and a depth rule (page and one
  panel; no box in a box).
- **Components:** use the `os-*` classes instead of rebuilding them; new
  `os-row`, `os-chip`, `os-link`, `os-danger`, `os-sheet`, `os-empty`; rules for
  buttons, sheets, page heads, lists, numbers and charts.
- **States** (new): loading, empty, error, saved, unsaved.
- **Patterns** 11–14 added: the answer is on the card, the next thing first,
  one fact in one place, a choice takes effect on what depends on it.
- **Motion:** durations and one easing. **Writing:** one date shape, a German
  glossary, buttons name the result. **Accessibility:** concrete focus ring,
  44 px targets, dialogs. **Review checklist** (new), seven points.
- Ben's rules are unchanged.
